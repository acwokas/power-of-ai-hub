# Global Rules for you.withthepowerof.ai

These rules apply to all pages, components, buttons, tooltips, helper text, empty states, error messages, and upgrade prompts.

---

## Tone Principles

- Calm
- Clear
- Adult
- Non-salesy
- Never breathless
- Never apologetic
- Never patronising

Use British English spelling.

Do not use em dashes.

Avoid hype, evangelism, or generic AI claims.

Do not use phrases such as:
- Democratising AI (as a headline or CTA)
- AI for everyone
- Unlock magic
- Instant results
- Growth hacks

The philosophy may reference democratising access to high quality thinking only in About or vision contexts.

---

## Button Copy Rules

### Approved primary button language:
- Explore the ecosystem
- Learn more
- Continue
- View options
- Review inputs
- Refine approach
- See examples

### Disallowed button language:
- Get started
- Try now
- Generate instantly
- Unlock power
- Boost growth

---

## Tooltips and Helper Text

Tooltips must:
- Explain what will happen next
- Set expectations clearly
- Assume an intelligent user

Example:
> "This will use your inputs to suggest a positioning direction. You can revise it later."

Avoid casual language, emojis, or exclamation marks.

---

## Empty States

Empty states must:
- Explain why the space is empty
- Suggest a thoughtful next step
- Reinforce clarity before action

Example:
> "There is nothing here yet because no inputs have been provided.
> When you are ready, start by outlining your current situation."

---

## Limit and Restriction States

Limits must feel intentional and protective, not punitive.

Example:
> "This view shows one positioning direction.
> Additional versions are available on paid plans so you can compare and refine."

Avoid urgency or pressure language.

---

## Error States

Errors must:
- State what happened
- Explain what to do next
- Avoid blame

Example:
> "We could not process this input.
> Please review it and try again."

---

## Free vs Paid Language Rules

### When referring to free access:
- Use "Free to start" instead of "Free"
- Emphasise exploration, not completeness

### When referring to paid access:
- Describe what becomes possible, not what is locked
- Reference continuity, history, and depth

### Avoid:
- "Upgrade now"
- "Unlock"
- "Paywall"
- "Limited time"

---

## Ecosystem Navigation and Language Rules

### Navigation:
- you.withthepowerof.ai is the framing layer, not a product
- Sub-sites may link back to the ecosystem but must retain their own identity
- Perform must never be positioned as a beginner or default option
- Experimental or shop properties must not appear as primary CTAs

### Language:
- Avoid hype, evangelism, and generic AI claims
- Do not use "democratising AI" as a headline or CTA
- Use "democratising access to high quality thinking" only in About or philosophy contexts

### Hierarchy:
1. **Discover** - establishes context
2. **Prompt** - supports thinking
3. **Play** - supports thinking
4. **Learn** - builds capability
5. **Build** - enables application
6. **Perform** - represents execution and outcomes

All future pages or products must map explicitly to this hierarchy before being added.

---

These rules override all default platform language.
