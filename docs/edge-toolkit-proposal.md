# EDGE toolkit proposal

11 September 2026. For Adrian's review. Proposed architecture, not approved route changes or a release.

## Purpose

Help people turn a real situation into a considered action, then learn from the result. EDGE is applicable across work, study, independent projects and everyday decisions. AI supports the thinking; it does not decide or certify the outcome.

Keep Evaluate, Define, Govern and Elevate. Start at Evaluate for a full journey, but allow entry at any stage. Depth should reflect the task. A short message should not require a project-management workflow.

## Six core experiences

| Experience | What the user needs | Result they can use | Existing tools and proposed changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decision Explorer (working name) | Compare a choice or strengthen a plan | Options, known facts, assumptions, trade-offs, evidence gaps and a reversible test | Evolve Decision Simulation. Add Challenge my plan as a mode using Red Team. Support more than two options without overwhelming the initial form. |
| Before You Send | Communicate a clear intention | Original alongside editable suggestion, supported observations and an honest explanation of changes | Keep as a distinct quick-entry tool. Preserve facts, conditions and uncertainty. A good message can remain unchanged. |
| Conversation Practice (working name) | Prepare for an interaction | A rehearsal, feedback tied to actual dialogue and a next-attempt plan | Combine Conversation and Negotiation in one engine, retaining distinct entry modes. Add governance/board-review rehearsal as a specialised scenario. Negotiation retains offers, limits, concessions and walk-away conditions. |
| Outcome Brief (new) | Define what they are trying to achieve | A one-page brief covering outcome, audience, success evidence, scope, constraints and open decisions | Fill the Define gap. Avoid turning an unclear request directly into a polished plan. Let the user confirm assumptions. |
| Action Plan (new) | Move forward with appropriate accountability | Next steps, proposed owners, dependencies, boundaries, escalation triggers and review point | Connect Govern and Elevate. Offer solo and team versions. Owners and deadlines are proposals until confirmed. Include a stakeholder/values check; do not make universal legal-compliance claims. |
| Progress Review (new) | Learn from what happened | Expected versus observed results, evidence, changes to make and the next experiment | Fill the Elevate gap. Let users record observations without AI. AI may help reflect, but must not invent results, causation or progress scores. |

The core has six visible entry points, not six mandatory steps. Working names require approval. Each experience can contribute to more than one pillar.

## Disposition of all twelve existing tools

1. Decision Simulation: evolve into Decision Explorer. Preserve its current address until migration is agreed.
2. Red Team Simulation: merge the underlying experience into a Challenge my plan mode. Preserve a focused entry page for people who arrive with a plan rather than a choice.
3. Before You Send: retain independently and connect to other outputs only when useful.
4. Conversation Simulator: become the common practice engine.
5. Negotiation Simulator: become a distinct mode of Conversation Practice, with negotiation-specific state and feedback. Do not flatten into a generic chatbot.
6. Governance Review: move the board/committee rehearsal into Conversation Practice. Put practical ownership and escalation outputs into Action Plan. Keep AI-specific review as a labelled specialist scenario.
7. Ethical Dilemma: retain a specialist learning exercise. Its generated scenarios, facts and outcomes must be explicitly fictional. Bring a short stakeholder-and-values check into real planning; do not generate real policies from five simulated choices.
8. Maturity Assessment: retain as an explicitly AI-focused self-assessment. Explain scoring and limitations. Do not imply certification, benchmarking or validated organisational maturity without evidence.
9. Brand Profile: connect with Brand Palette in a specialist Brand and Content workspace. Existing profile-to-content-sprint transfer is worth preserving.
10. Brand Palette: a module in that workspace, with focused direct access for people who only need palette exports.
11. Content Sprint: part of the same specialist workspace. Preserve standalone task entry. Track intended audience and outcomes rather than generate a quota of posts by default.
12. Prompt Engineer: treat as a supporting utility and assess consolidation with PromptAndGo. Verify PromptAndGo's actual capabilities before choosing a canonical home or redirecting anything.

Retirement is a product decision, not deletion by default. No usage evidence has been examined in this pass, so there is no claim that any current tool lacks demand.

## Connected work without compulsory accounts

Create an editable EDGE brief that carries context between tools: situation, desired outcome, supplied facts and sources, assumptions, options, chosen direction, affected people, actions, owners and observed results.

Keep supplied facts, AI suggestions and user-confirmed decisions visibly distinct. A hypothesis must never silently become a fact in a later tool. Before each handoff, show the user what will be carried forward and allow editing or removal. Do not send all previous conversations by default.

For quick tasks, keep the current session lightweight. Offer explicit local save plus an export/import option first. Describe that a saved browser draft remains on the device. Cross-device accounts, history and optional reminders come later, with deletion and retention controls designed before storage is introduced.

## User journeys

### A student organising a project

Evaluate options for the event format. Define the intended result and audience in an Outcome Brief. Agree responsibilities and spending boundaries in Action Plan. Review attendance, feedback and lessons after the event. Use Before You Send for the invitation if needed.

### A manager changing a service

Compare a pilot and rollout in Decision Explorer. Challenge the preferred plan. Carry confirmed direction into Outcome Brief and Action Plan. Rehearse the team conversation. Review actual delivery time and customer feedback before expanding.

### Someone preparing an important message

Open Before You Send, review the wording, edit and copy it. Finish there. Offer Conversation Practice only when the user needs to discuss the topic live.

## Discovery and accessibility

Lead with 'What would you like to do?' and plain-language tasks. Show the associated EDGE pillars as guidance, with a separate full-framework view. Avoid making visitors learn the acronym before receiving value.

Offer a quick route with a few essential inputs and an optional deeper route. Include examples from students, individuals, community initiatives, small organisations and established businesses. Use mobile-friendly forms, keyboard operation, labelled controls, readable contrasts and user-controlled streaming announcements. Test with real users rather than assume that simpler copy means universal usability.

Use optional audience/location context where relevant. Do not infer culture from nationality or use cultural stereotypes as behavioural predictions.

## Access and success measures

Keep first useful output, copy and basic export open. Optional accounts should earn their place through saved work and continuity. Marketing consent is a separate, unselected choice. Avoid gates that interrupt someone trying to resolve a difficult situation.

Measure starts, useful completions, edits, exports, user-chosen handoffs, return sessions and voluntary outcome feedback. Do not put raw messages or sensitive project context in analytics. Collect optional 'Was this useful?' feedback with a reason, not only a rating. Prefer evidence of action and return value over time spent in the tool.

## Build order and release gates

1. Agree this product map and confirm working names.
2. Prototype one connected journey: Decision Explorer -> Outcome Brief -> Action Plan -> Progress Review. Reuse approved visual language. Demonstrate with a labelled fictional case.
3. Consolidate Conversation Practice and its modes. Keep Before You Send as a quick standalone tool.
4. Group and reassess specialist applications.
5. Add optional persistence/accounts only after the continuity benefit is demonstrated.

Before release: test grounded and incomplete inputs, clear/no-change cases, contradictions, source-versus-assumption handling, multi-option choices, stream failures, accessibility, mobile layouts and edited exports. Verify processing notices against actual provider/storage behaviour. Add service abuse controls. No broad legal-compliance promises.

Migration must inventory existing links, canonical URLs, sitemap entries, analytics events, personal-site tool links, EDGE white paper and LinkedIn Featured references. Use redirects only where the replacement genuinely fulfils the old task. Do not change framework ownership or create another domain.

## Current implementation state

Before You Send and Decision Simulation have preview upgrades and limited live quality tests. Browser/mobile/export checks remain incomplete. Red Team backend edits are local and unfinished after the product-direction pause; do not describe them as released or deploy them without completing and testing the integration. Production is unchanged by this proposal.

## Specialist reassessment, 11 September follow-up

Brand and Content now has a local four-stage working brief and selected-context handoff to the planner. Brand Profile and Palette remain standalone while their integration is developed. Only selected context travels to the planner, with explicit recipient acceptance. Full brief retained in same-tab session storage for return within two hours. Browser verified excluded notes stayed out of recipient fields and restored on return.

Content planning now uses author-chosen idea count, outcome and evidence instead of assuming daily publishing. Actual model compliance with this new brief still needs live sampling. Existing automatic local result storage, stream recovery and full API validation need further work before release.

Ethics practice: keep it as a fictional learning application. Prompts now distinguish hypothetical reactions from actual evidence and avoid deriving permitted autonomy or approved policies from user choices. Preserve existing output headings temporarily for parser compatibility. Frontend labels and exports still require complete alignment and live output evaluation.

Maturity questionnaire: rework as an evidence-led AI readiness conversation, not a diagnostic rating. Current self-report averages, maturity tier descriptions and PDF make stronger conclusions than the evidence supports. Replace the percentage/ranking with areas evidenced, unknowns and next actions. Keep useful ownership, monitoring and escalation questions, allow not applicable with reasons, and ask for supporting evidence. Small organisations should not be treated as less capable merely because they lack formal committees. Screen and downloadable report must change together before release.
